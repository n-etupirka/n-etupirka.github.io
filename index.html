About Me

I am a penetration tester and security consultant at a cybersecurity company in Japan.

I specialize in offensive security, primarily conducting penetration tests of enterprise environments and external and internal networks. I am also involved in threat-informed security assessments, including red team exercises and Threat-Led Penetration Testing (TLPT).

In addition, my work includes researching and evaluating offensive security techniques, developing and evaluating security tools, writing technical articles, contributing to security guidelines, and delivering security training.
